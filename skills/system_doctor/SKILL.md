# Finds what went wrong on this computer since boot (failed services, errors grouped by source, kernel warnings, out-of-memory kills, crashes, boot time) and shows it as a report.

Use it when I ask why something is broken, slow or crashing, or for a health
check. Call `system_doctor.check` once; the report appears in the side panel by
itself. Then explain the most important problems in plain words and suggest
fixes. Don't repeat the numbers the report already shows.
